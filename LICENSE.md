# Licences

Each law pack in `packs/` is an unofficial copy of official law texts, made word for word from the official source named in its `pack.json`. Each pack carries the licence of that source, given in its `pack.json` under `source.licence`:

| Pack | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `eu-gdpr`, `eu-eprivacy`, `eu-dsa`, `eu-data-act`, `eu-ai-act` | Publications Office of the European Union | Reuse authorised, source acknowledged (Commission Decision 2011/833/EU) |
| `fi` | Finlex (Ministry of Justice, Finland) | CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `de` | gesetze-im-internet.de (Federal Ministry of Justice) | Official work without copyright (§ 5 UrhG) |
| `nl` | wetten.overheid.nl (KOOP) | No copyright (Auteurswet article 11) |
| `at` | RIS (Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes, Bundeskanzleramt) | CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `se` | Riksdagen (data.riksdagen.se) | No copyright (upphovsrättslagen 1 kap. 9 §) |

Only the official publication of a law is legally binding.
