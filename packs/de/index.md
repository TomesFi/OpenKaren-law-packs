# Germany: data protection, spam and cookie laws

## Teil 1 — Gemeinsame Bestimmungen
### Kapitel 1 — Anwendungsbereich und Begriffsbestimmungen
- bdsg/sec_001.md | § 1 | Anwendungsbereich des Gesetzes | This section defines the scope of the German Federal Data Protection Act, specifying which public and private entities and data processing activities it covers, its territorial reach, and its relationship to EU laws such as the GDPR. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_002.md | § 2 | Begriffsbestimmungen | This section defines which entities count as public bodies of the Federation and the Länder, private-law associations performing public administration tasks, and non-public bodies, including when public bodies are treated as non-public while participating in competition. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
### Kapitel 2 — Rechtsgrundlagen der Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten
- bdsg/sec_003.md | § 3 | Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten durch öffentliche Stellen | This section states that public authorities may process personal data when it is necessary to carry out a task within their responsibility or to exercise the public authority vested in them.
- bdsg/sec_004.md | § 4 | Videoüberwachung öffentlich zugänglicher Räume | This section sets out when video surveillance of publicly accessible areas is permitted under German data protection law, along with requirements for notifying the public, storing footage, and deleting recordings. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
### Kapitel 3 — Datenschutzbeauftragte öffentlicher Stellen
- bdsg/sec_005.md | § 5 | Benennung | This section covers the rules for public bodies appointing a data protection officer, including shared appointments, required expertise, whether the officer is an employee or external contractor, and the obligation to publish their contact details to the Federal Commissioner.
- bdsg/sec_006.md | § 6 | Stellung | This section defines the status, independence, protection from dismissal, support entitlements, and confidentiality and professional secrecy rights of the Data Protection Officer in public bodies.
- bdsg/sec_007.md | § 7 | Aufgaben | This section outlines the specific tasks of the data protection officer in public bodies, including advising employees, monitoring compliance, cooperating with the supervisory authority, and ensuring a risk-based approach, with a note that these duties do not extend to a court's judicial activities.
### Kapitel 4 — Die oder der Bundesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit
- bdsg/sec_008.md | § 8 | Errichtung | This section establishes the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information as a supreme federal authority headquartered in Bonn, designates its employees as federal civil servants, and allows the delegation of personnel administration tasks to other federal bodies provided the Commissioner's independence is maintained and only necessary employee data is shared.
- bdsg/sec_009.md | § 9 | Zuständigkeit | This section states that the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection oversees federal public bodies, certain telecommunications companies, and federal-majority-owned processors, but not federal courts when they process data as part of their judicial work.
- bdsg/sec_010.md | § 10 | Unabhängigkeit | This section states that the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection performs their duties and exercises their powers with complete independence, free from any external influence or instructions, and is subject to audit by the Federal Court of Auditors only to the extent that such audit does not compromise that independence.
- bdsg/sec_011.md | § 11 | Ernennung und Amtszeit | This section covers how the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection is elected and appointed, the qualifications required, the oath of office, and the five-year term of office with one possible re-election.
- bdsg/sec_012.md | § 12 | Amtsverhältnis | This section sets out the official status, appointment, term of office, dismissal, deputy arrangements, and remuneration of the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
- bdsg/sec_013.md | § 13 | Rechte und Pflichten | This section sets out the Federal Data Protection Commissioner's rules on independence and conflicts of interest, reporting of gifts, confidentiality, the right to refuse testimony and document production, and giving evidence as a witness, with corresponding application to state data protection authorities.
- bdsg/sec_014.md | § 14 | Aufgaben | This section lists the duties of the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, including monitoring data protection compliance, advising government bodies, handling complaints, and cooperating with other supervisory authorities. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_015.md | § 15 | Tätigkeitsbericht | This section requires the German Federal Commissioner for Data Protection to publish an annual activity report listing types of reported violations and enforcement measures taken, and to submit it to the German parliament, federal government, EU institutions, and the public.
- bdsg/sec_016.md | § 16 | Befugnisse | This section defines the investigative and enforcement powers of the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection, including the right to access premises and data, object to violations by federal authorities, warn of unlawful processing, and cooperate with state supervisory bodies. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
### Kapitel 5 — Vertretung im Europäischen Datenschutzausschuss, zentrale Anlaufstelle, Zusammenarbeit der Aufsichtsbehörden des Bundes und der Länder in Angelegenheiten der Europäischen Union
- bdsg/sec_017.md | § 17 | Vertretung im Europäischen Datenschutzausschuss, zentrale Anlaufstelle | This section designates the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection as Germany's common representative in the European Data Protection Board and central contact point, provides for a deputy elected by the Bundesrat from among the heads of state data protection authorities, and specifies that the deputy may assume negotiation and voting rights in matters falling within the states' exclusive legislative competence or concerning state authorities.
- bdsg/sec_018.md | § 18 | Verfahren der Zusammenarbeit der Aufsichtsbehörden des Bundes und der Länder | This section sets out how Germany's federal and state data protection authorities cooperate, exchange information, and determine a common position when engaging with EU institutions on data protection matters.
- bdsg/sec_019.md | § 19 | Zuständigkeiten | This section specifies which German supervisory authority acts as the lead authority in GDPR cooperation and consistency procedures and how complaints are forwarded between supervisory authorities. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
### Kapitel 6 — Rechtsbehelfe
- bdsg/sec_020.md | § 20 | Gerichtlicher Rechtsschutz | This section establishes the administrative court procedure for disputes between individuals or legal entities and data protection supervisory authorities concerning rights under Article 78 of the GDPR and § 61, including rules on jurisdiction, parties, and the exclusion of preliminary proceedings. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_021.md | § 21 | Antrag der Aufsichtsbehörde auf gerichtliche Entscheidung bei angenommener Rechtswidrigkeit eines Beschlusses der Europäischen Kommission | This section sets out the procedure by which a German supervisory authority must suspend its own proceedings and apply to the Federal Administrative Court for a ruling on the validity of a European Commission decision (such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses) that it considers unlawful, including the possibility of a referral to the Court of Justice of the European Union.

- bdsg/sec_023.md | § 23 | Verarbeitung zu anderen Zwecken durch öffentliche Stellen | This section sets out when public authorities may process personal data for a different purpose than the one for which it was originally collected, including additional conditions for special categories of data.

- bdsg/sec_028.md | § 28 | Datenverarbeitung zu im öffentlichen Interesse liegenden Archivzwecken | This section covers the rules for processing personal data for archiving purposes in the public interest, including when special categories of data may be used and which data subject rights (such as access, rectification, and objection) are limited or excluded. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_029.md | § 29 | Rechte der betroffenen Person und aufsichtsbehördliche Befugnisse im Fall von Geheimhaltungspflichten | This section sets out the exceptions to data subjects' rights to information, access, and breach notification, and to supervisory authorities' investigative powers, where exercising them would disclose information that must be kept secret by law or due to overriding legitimate interests, including special protections for professional secrecy holders. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_030.md | § 30 | Verbraucherkredite | This section governs how credit reference agencies must handle information requests from lenders in other EU countries and requires lenders to notify consumers when a loan is denied based on credit data, except where public safety is at risk. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_031.md | § 31 | Schutz des Wirtschaftsverkehrs bei Scoring und Bonitätsauskünften | This section sets out when it is lawful to use a scoring value (a probability about a person's future conduct) to decide whether to start, continue, or end a contract with them, and which unpaid debts a credit agency may include when calculating a creditworthiness score. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
### Kapitel 2 — Rechte der betroffenen Person
- bdsg/sec_032.md | § 32 | Informationspflicht bei Erhebung von personenbezogenen Daten bei der betroffenen Person | It lists the specific situations in which the duty to inform a data subject about intended further processing of directly collected personal data does not apply, the protective measures required when that information is withheld, and the deadline for providing it once a temporary obstacle is removed. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_033.md | § 33 | Informationspflicht, wenn die personenbezogenen Daten nicht bei der betroffenen Person erhoben wurden | This section lists the exceptions to the requirement to inform individuals when their personal data was not collected from them directly, the alternative safeguards controllers must apply when withholding that information, and the special consent requirement for disclosures to security and intelligence agencies. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_034.md | § 34 | Auskunftsrecht der betroffenen Person | This section explains when a person's right to access their personal data under EU GDPR Article 15 does not apply, how refusals must be documented and justified, when the Federal Data Protection Commissioner must be involved, and the limits on accessing data that is not stored in a filing system. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_035.md | § 35 | Recht auf Löschung | This section explains when the right to erasure is satisfied by restricting processing instead of deleting data, covering cases where deletion is impractical for non-automated processing, would impair the data subject's interests, or conflicts with retention periods. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_036.md | § 36 | Widerspruchsrecht | This section states that the right to object to data processing by a public authority does not apply when a compelling public interest outweighs the individual's interests or when a legal provision requires the processing. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
- bdsg/sec_037.md | § 37 | Automatisierte Entscheidungen im Einzelfall einschließlich Profiling | This section sets out the exceptions that allow automated decisions in insurance contracts despite the general right to object to such decisions, and the specific safeguards required when those decisions involve health data. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify

### Kapitel 5 — Sanktionen

- bdsg/sec_044.md | § 44 | Klagen gegen den Verantwortlichen oder Auftragsverarbeiter | This section establishes that data subjects may sue controllers or processors for GDPR violations at the court of the controller's establishment or the data subject's habitual residence, excludes public authorities acting in an official capacity from this rule, and provides that an appointed EU representative is authorized to receive service of process in such civil proceedings. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify
## Teil 3 — Bestimmungen für Verarbeitungen zu Zwecken gemäß Artikel 1 Absatz 1 der Richtlinie (EU) 2016/680
### Kapitel 1 — Anwendungsbereich, Begriffsbestimmungen und allgemeine Grundsätze für die Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten
- bdsg/sec_045.md | § 45 | Anwendungsbereich | This section specifies which public authorities and data processors are covered by the special data protection rules when handling personal data for the prevention, investigation, prosecution, and punishment of criminal and administrative offenses, as well as for the enforcement of sentences, measures, and fines.
- bdsg/sec_046.md | § 46 | Begriffsbestimmungen | This section defines key terms used in German data protection law, such as personal data, processing, controller, processor, and consent.
- bdsg/sec_047.md | § 47 | Allgemeine Grundsätze für die Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten | This section lists the six general principles that personal data must satisfy when processed, covering lawful and fair handling, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, and security.
### Kapitel 2 — Rechtsgrundlagen der Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten
- bdsg/sec_048.md | § 48 | Verarbeitung besonderer Kategorien personenbezogener Daten | This section states that special categories of personal data may only be processed when strictly necessary and lists the specific safeguards—such as encryption, pseudonymization, access restrictions, and separate storage—that must be implemented to protect individuals' rights.
- bdsg/sec_049.md | § 49 | Verarbeitung zu anderen Zwecken | This section states the conditions under which personal data may be processed for a purpose other than the one for which it was originally collected.
- bdsg/sec_050.md | § 50 | Verarbeitung zu archivarischen, wissenschaftlichen und statistischen Zwecken | This section permits the processing of personal data for archival, scientific, or statistical purposes when there is a public interest and suitable safeguards—such as prompt anonymization, protection against unauthorized access, or separation from other processing activities—are in place.
- bdsg/sec_051.md | § 51 | Einwilligung | This section sets out the rules for obtaining, proving, and withdrawing consent to process personal data, including how consent must be requested, the right to revoke it, and special requirements for sensitive data categories.
- bdsg/sec_052.md | § 52 | Verarbeitung auf Weisung des Verantwortlichen | This provision states that any person working under a data controller or processor may process personal data only on the controller's instructions, unless a legal provision requires them to do so.
- bdsg/sec_053.md | § 53 | Datengeheimnis | Anyone who works with personal data must keep it secret, must promise to do so when they start, and must keep keeping it secret even after they leave.
- bdsg/sec_054.md | § 54 | Automatisierte Einzelentscheidung | This section sets out when decisions based solely on automated processing are allowed, restricts their use of special categories of personal data unless safeguards are in place, and prohibits profiling that discriminates based on those special categories.
### Kapitel 3 — Rechte der betroffenen Person
- bdsg/sec_055.md | § 55 | Allgemeine Informationen zu Datenverarbeitungen | This section requires data controllers to make publicly available general information about their processing of personal data, including the purposes of processing, data subjects' rights, the contact details of the controller and data protection officer, and how to reach the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection.
- bdsg/sec_056.md | § 56 | Benachrichtigung betroffener Personen | This section sets out the required content of notifications to affected individuals about the processing of their personal data, the grounds for delaying or withholding such notifications, and the special consent rules for notifications involving security and intelligence agencies.
- bdsg/sec_057.md | § 57 | Auskunftsrecht | This section sets out the right of individuals to request information from data controllers about the processing of their personal data, including the specific details they must receive, the circumstances under which this right may be refused or restricted, and the procedures for informing them of such decisions and involving the Federal Commissioner.
- bdsg/sec_058.md | § 58 | Rechte auf Berichtigung und Löschung sowie Einschränkung der Verarbeitung | This section outlines individuals' rights to request correction, completion, or deletion of their personal data, or restriction of its processing in certain cases, along with the controller's duties to notify recipients and inform the data subject.
- bdsg/sec_059.md | § 59 | Verfahren für die Ausübung der Rechte der betroffenen Person | This section describes the procedures data controllers must follow when individuals exercise their data protection rights, covering communication standards, response requirements, fees, and identity verification.
- bdsg/sec_060.md | § 60 | Anrufung der oder des Bundesbeauftragten | This section describes how individuals can complain to the Federal Data Protection Commissioner about personal data processing by public bodies for the purposes listed in § 45 (except when courts process data in their judicial role), how the Commissioner must report back on the complaint and point to court remedies, and how complaints about processing in other EU countries are forwarded to the relevant foreign authority.
- bdsg/sec_061.md | § 61 | Rechtsschutz gegen Entscheidungen der oder des Bundesbeauftragten oder bei deren oder dessen Untätigkeit | This section allows individuals and organizations to take legal action in court against binding decisions made by the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection, or against the Commissioner's failure to handle a complaint or inform the complainant of the outcome within three months.
### Kapitel 4 — Pflichten der Verantwortlichen und Auftragsverarbeiter
- bdsg/sec_062.md | § 62 | Auftragsverarbeitung | This section sets out the rules for when a controller has personal data processed by another party on its behalf, including the controller's responsibility for compliance, requirements for selecting processors, rules on using sub-processors, the mandatory contents of the processing contract, and when a processor is treated as a controller.
- bdsg/sec_063.md | § 63 | Gemeinsam Verantwortliche | This part explains that when two or more parties jointly decide how personal data is processed, they must agree in writing on their respective data protection duties, but individuals can still assert their rights against any one of them.
- bdsg/sec_064.md | § 64 | Anforderungen an die Sicherheit der Datenverarbeitung | This section outlines the technical and organizational measures that controllers and processors must implement to ensure a risk-appropriate level of security for personal data processing, including specific controls for automated systems such as encryption, access restrictions, and logging.
- bdsg/sec_065.md | § 65 | Meldung von Verletzungen des Schutzes personenbezogener Daten an die oder den Bundesbeauftragten | This section sets out the obligation to report personal data breaches to the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection within 72 hours, the information such reports must contain, documentation requirements, and rules for breaches involving data transferred to or from other EU member states.
- bdsg/sec_066.md | § 66 | Benachrichtigung betroffener Personen bei Verletzungen des Schutzes personenbezogener Daten | This section sets out when data controllers must notify affected individuals about personal data breaches, what the notification must contain, and the circumstances under which notification may be waived, delayed, or replaced by a public notice.
- bdsg/sec_067.md | § 67 | Durchführung einer Datenschutz-Folgenabschätzung | This section sets out the requirements for data controllers to conduct a data protection impact assessment when processing is likely to pose high risks to individuals, including what the assessment must contain, who must participate, and how compliance is checked.
- bdsg/sec_068.md | § 68 | Zusammenarbeit mit der oder dem Bundesbeauftragten | This section states that data controllers must cooperate with the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection when the Commissioner is carrying out their duties.
- bdsg/sec_069.md | § 69 | Anhörung der oder des Bundesbeauftragten | This section requires data controllers to consult the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection before putting new high-risk file systems into operation, specifies the information and documents to be submitted, and details the Commissioner's power to issue recommendations within set deadlines, including an exception for urgent cases.
- bdsg/sec_070.md | § 70 | Verzeichnis von Verarbeitungstätigkeiten | This section requires data controllers and processors to maintain detailed written or electronic records of their processing activities—including purposes, data categories, recipients, legal bases, and security measures—and to make these records available to the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection upon request.
- bdsg/sec_071.md | § 71 | Datenschutz durch Technikgestaltung und datenschutzfreundliche Voreinstellungen | This section requires data controllers to implement data protection by design and by default, ensuring that systems minimize personal data and that default settings process only the data necessary for each specific purpose.
- bdsg/sec_072.md | § 72 | Unterscheidung zwischen verschiedenen Kategorien betroffener Personen | This section requires data controllers to distinguish between different categories of data subjects—such as criminal suspects, convicted offenders, victims, witnesses, and persons connected to them—when processing personal data.
- bdsg/sec_073.md | § 73 | Unterscheidung zwischen Tatsachen und persönlichen Einschätzungen | This section requires data controllers to distinguish between personal data based on facts and data based on personal opinions, to mark opinion-based assessments as such where feasible, and to make it possible to identify which entity holds the documents underlying those opinions.
- bdsg/sec_074.md | § 74 | Verfahren bei Übermittlungen | This section sets out the rules for transmitting personal data, including checking that data is accurate and up to date before sending it, telling the recipient about any special conditions that apply, and not applying stricter rules to recipients in other EU countries than to domestic recipients.
- bdsg/sec_075.md | § 75 | Berichtigung und Löschung personenbezogener Daten sowie Einschränkung der Verarbeitung | This section requires data controllers to correct inaccurate personal data, delete it when processing is unlawful or no longer necessary, notify recipients of corrections or unlawful disclosures, and set and enforce appropriate deadlines for deletion or regular review of stored data.
- bdsg/sec_076.md | § 76 | Protokollierung | This section requires controllers and processors to log specified data processing operations in automated systems, details what those logs must record and how they may be used, and sets rules for their retention and disclosure to the Federal Commissioner.
- bdsg/sec_077.md | § 77 | Vertrauliche Meldung von Verstößen | This section requires data controllers to allow confidential reports of data protection violations occurring in their area of responsibility to be sent to them.
### Kapitel 5 — Datenübermittlungen an Drittstaaten und an internationale Organisationen
- bdsg/sec_078.md | § 78 | Allgemeine Voraussetzungen | This section sets out the rules for transferring personal data to third countries or international organizations for law enforcement purposes, including when an EU adequacy decision is required, when transfers must be refused despite such a decision, when prior approval from another EU member state is needed, and how onward transfers to other countries must be authorized.
- bdsg/sec_079.md | § 79 | Datenübermittlung bei geeigneten Garantien | This provision governs the transfer of personal data to third countries or international organizations in the absence of an adequacy decision when appropriate safeguards are provided, and specifies the documentation and annual reporting duties that apply to transfers based on the controller's own assessment.
- bdsg/sec_080.md | § 80 | Datenübermittlung ohne geeignete Garantien | This section lists the specific exceptions permitting personal data transfers to third countries or international organizations without an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards—such as protecting vital interests, averting threats to public security, or handling legal claims—and states that such transfers must be withheld when the data subject's fundamental rights outweigh the public interest.
- bdsg/sec_081.md | § 81 | Sonstige Datenübermittlung an Empfänger in Drittstaaten | This section describes the special individual cases in which controllers may directly transfer personal data to recipients in third countries when absolutely necessary for their tasks, including requirements to notify certain authorities and ensure recipients only use the data for the specified purpose.
### Kapitel 6 — Zusammenarbeit der Aufsichtsbehörden
- bdsg/sec_082.md | § 82 | Gegenseitige Amtshilfe | This section sets out the rules for the German Federal Data Protection Commissioner to provide mutual assistance and exchange information with data protection authorities in other EU member states to ensure consistent application of EU Directive 2016/680, including deadlines for responses, grounds for refusal, reporting obligations, and cost arrangements.
### Kapitel 7 — Haftung und Sanktionen
- bdsg/sec_083.md | § 83 | Schadensersatz und Entschädigung | This section sets out the rules for when a data controller must pay damages or compensation to a person harmed by unlawful processing of their personal data, covering financial and non-financial loss, shared liability among multiple controllers, reductions for the victim's own fault, and deadlines for claims.
- bdsg/sec_084.md | § 84 | Strafvorschriften | This provision makes the criminal penalties in Section 42 apply to public authorities processing personal data for the activities described in Section 45.
## Teil 4 — Besondere Bestimmungen für Verarbeitungen im Rahmen von nicht in die Anwendungsbereiche der Verordnung (EU) 2016/679 und der Richtlinie (EU) 2016/680 fallenden Tätigkeiten

## Teil 2 — Datenschutz und Schutz der Privatsphäre in der Telekommunikation
### Kapitel 1 — Vertraulichkeit der Kommunikation
- tdddg/sec_003.md | § 3 | Vertraulichkeit der Kommunikation – Fernmeldegeheimnis | This section establishes the secrecy of telecommunications, covering what content and circumstances are protected, which providers and operators must maintain confidentiality, and the restrictions on obtaining and using such information.
- tdddg/sec_004.md | § 4 | Rechte des Erben des Endnutzers und anderer berechtigter Personen | This provision clarifies that telecommunications secrecy does not block heirs or other authorized persons from exercising the end user's rights against the telecommunications service provider.
- tdddg/sec_005.md | § 5 | Abhörverbot, Geheimhaltungspflicht der Betreiber von Funkanlagen | This section restricts the interception of radio communications to those intended for the operator or the public and imposes a confidentiality obligation on anyone who receives other private messages, while leaving legally authorized interception unaffected.
- tdddg/sec_006.md | § 6 | Nachrichtenübermittlung mit Zwischenspeicherung | This section specifies when telecommunications providers may temporarily store and process users' message content for transmission and requires them to implement technical and organizational measures to prevent misdelivery and unauthorized disclosure of that content.
- tdddg/sec_007.md | § 7 | Verlangen eines amtlichen Ausweises | This section covers when telecom providers can demand an official or electronic ID to verify customer details when setting up or changing a service contract, and the requirement that any copy of the ID be destroyed immediately after the necessary information is recorded.
- tdddg/sec_008.md | § 8 | Missbrauch von Telekommunikationsanlagen | This section prohibits possessing, manufacturing, selling, importing, or advertising telecommunications equipment disguised as everyday items that is designed to secretly record others' speech or images, and lists exceptions for certain authorized holders, permanently disabled devices, official use, and approved exports.
### Kapitel 2 — Verkehrsdaten, Standortdaten
- tdddg/sec_009.md | § 9 | Verarbeitung von Verkehrsdaten | This section sets out the rules for when telecommunications providers may process traffic data such as connection numbers, timestamps, and location information for technical and billing purposes, requires immediate deletion after the connection ends, and allows further use for marketing or service design only with the user's consent.
- tdddg/sec_010.md | § 10 | Entgeltermittlung und Entgeltabrechnung | This section specifies how telecommunications providers may process traffic data to calculate charges and issue bills to end users and other providers, including storage limits and conditions for sharing data with third parties for billing or debt collection.
- tdddg/sec_011.md | § 11 | Einzelverbindungsnachweis | This section governs when telecommunications providers must provide end users with itemized records of their chargeable connections, including rules on truncating phone numbers, notifying household members and employees, and protecting calls to confidential counseling services.
- tdddg/sec_012.md | § 12 | Störungen von Telekommunikationsanlagen und Missbrauch von Telekommunikationsdiensten | This section governs when telecommunications providers may process traffic and control data to detect, limit, or remedy faults and disruptions in their networks, monitor connections for operational purposes, and investigate suspected unlawful use of services, together with the related duties to delete data, notify affected users and authorities, and document such measures.
- tdddg/sec_013.md | § 13 | Standortdaten | This section governs the processing of location data of users of public telecommunications networks and services, covering consent and notification requirements, the right to temporarily prohibit processing, mandatory location transmission for emergency calls, and restrictions on who may access the data.
- tdddg/sec_013a.md | § 13a | Erfüllung von Pflichten gemäß den Artikeln 10 und 11 der Verordnung (EU) 2023/1543 in der Fassung vom 12. Juli 2023 | Telecommunications service providers may process personal data when required to comply with European production or preservation orders under EU Regulation 2023/1543, and this processing restricts the constitutional protection of telecommunications secrecy.
### Kapitel 3 — Mitteilen ankommender Verbindungen, Rufnummernanzeige und -unterdrückung, automatische Anrufweiterschaltung
- tdddg/sec_014.md | § 14 | Mitteilen ankommender Verbindungen | This section outlines the rules for telecommunications providers to disclose caller identification and connection data to subscribers who report threatening or harassing calls, covering cross-network cooperation, anti-abuse safeguards, notification to the caller, and supervisory oversight.
- tdddg/sec_015.md | § 15 | Rufnummernanzeige und -unterdrückung | This section sets out the rules for caller ID display and suppression on voice communication services, including free per-call or permanent blocking for callers and recipients, exceptions for emergency numbers, a requirement that marketing calls show the caller's number, and default suppression for subscribers not listed in the directory.
- tdddg/sec_016.md | § 16 | Automatische Anrufweiterschaltung | This section requires providers of voice communication services to let end users easily and at no cost disable automatic call forwarding initiated by a third party to their device, where technically possible.
### Kapitel 4 — Endnutzerverzeichnisse, Bereitstellen von Endnutzerdaten
- tdddg/sec_017.md | § 17 | Endnutzerverzeichnisse | This section sets out subscribers' rights to have their name, number, and address included in or removed from public end-user directories, and the duties of directory providers to publish, correct, and delete such entries.
- tdddg/sec_018.md | § 18 | Bereitstellen von Endnutzerdaten | This section covers the obligation of number-based interpersonal telecom service providers to provide end-user data to other companies upon request for use in public directories, call-notification services, and similar offerings, along with the rules on fees, non-discrimination, timeliness, and data formatting that apply to that data provision.
## Teil 3 — Datenschutz bei digitalen Diensten, Endeinrichtungen
### Kapitel 1 — Technische und organisatorische Vorkehrungen, Verarbeitung von Daten zum Zweck des Jugendschutzes und zur Auskunftserteilung
- tdddg/sec_019.md | § 19 | Technische und organisatorische Vorkehrungen | It sets out the technical and organizational security and privacy duties of digital-service providers, including letting users stop the service at any time, shielding use from third parties, offering anonymous or pseudonymous use and payment where feasible, flagging referrals to other providers, and protecting their systems against unauthorized access and disruption in line with the state of the art.

- tdddg/sec_022.md | § 22 | Auskunftsverfahren bei Bestandsdaten | This section governs when providers of digital services may hand over inventory data (such as user details, which can be identified via IP addresses) to designated government authorities like police, intelligence agencies, and customs for purposes including criminal investigations and public safety, and specifies the required request procedures and confidentiality obligations.
- tdddg/sec_023.md | § 23 | Auskunftsverfahren bei Passwörtern und anderen Zugangsdaten | This provision governs when digital service providers may hand over passwords and other access data to law enforcement or public safety authorities under court order for serious criminal prosecution or danger prevention, while prohibiting disclosure to other parties and requiring confidentiality and internal review.
- tdddg/sec_024.md | § 24 | Auskunftsverfahren bei Nutzungsdaten | This section sets out the rules for when and how providers of digital services must disclose usage data to law enforcement, security, and intelligence authorities, including the required legal basis, formal request procedures, and confidentiality obligations.

### Kapitel 2 — Endeinrichtungen

- tdddg/sec_026.md | § 26 | Anerkannte Dienste zur Einwilligungsverwaltung, Endnutzereinstellungen | This section establishes the criteria for recognizing independent consent management services, mandates that software and digital services respect end-user consent settings, and requires a government review of the measures' effectiveness.
