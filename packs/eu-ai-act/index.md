## CHAPTER I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
- art_001.md | Article 1 | Subject matter' | This part defines the overall purpose and scope of the AI Act, including its goals and the types of rules it establishes.
- art_002.md | Article 2 | Scope | This part defines who and what is covered by the AI Act, including exceptions and special cases.

- art_004a.md | Article 4a | Processing of special categories of personal data for bias detection and correction | This part covers the conditions under which special categories of personal data may be processed for detecting and correcting biases in AI systems.
## CHAPTER II — PROHIBITED AI PRACTICES
- art_005.md | Article 5 | Prohibited AI practices | This part covers the AI practices banned under the AI Act, including manipulative or deceptive techniques, exploitation of vulnerabilities, generating intimate or sexually explicit images of an identifiable person without consent, generating child sexual abuse material, social scoring, predicting criminal offences from profiling, untargeted scraping for facial recognition databases, emotion recognition at work and in education, biometric categorisation by sensitive traits, and real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces for law enforcement.
