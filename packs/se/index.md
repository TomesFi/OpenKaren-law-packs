## Inledande bestämmelse
- mfl/sec_001.md | 1 § | 1 § | This section states the purpose of the Swedish Marketing Act, which is to promote the interests of consumers and businesses in the marketing of products and to counteract unfair marketing practices.
## Lagens tillämpningsområde
- mfl/sec_002.md | 2 § | 2 § | This section defines when the Swedish Marketing Act applies, covering business marketing, broadcast and streaming media, and specific EU enforcement duties, while noting exceptions for services and marketing by traders established in other EEA countries.
## Definitioner
- mfl/sec_003.md | 3 § | 3 § | This section defines the key terms used throughout the Swedish Marketing Act, including consumer, trader, marketing, digital content, and products.
## Direktivbilagan
- mfl/sec_004.md | 4 § | 4 § | This section incorporates Annex I of EU Directive 2005/29/EC into Swedish law, making the listed unfair commercial practices prohibited in Sweden, extending most of them to business-to-business marketing, and carving out an exception for matters covered by the Gambling Act.
## God marknadsföringssed
- mfl/sec_005.md | 5 § | 5 § | This section states that all marketing must comply with good marketing practice.
- mfl/sec_006.md | 6 § | 6 § | This section states that marketing which violates good marketing practice is considered unfair when it noticeably affects or is likely to affect the recipient's ability to make an informed business decision.
## Aggressiv marknadsföring
- mfl/sec_007.md | 7 § | 7 § | This section prohibits traders from using aggressive marketing, such as harassment, coercion, physical violence, or threats, and defines when such marketing is considered unfair.
- mfl/sec_007a.md | 7 a § | 7 a § | This provision requires that the nature, timing, duration, and location of marketing be considered—along with specific conduct like threatening language, exploiting misfortune, obstructing contractual rights, or threatening illegal acts—when determining if the marketing is aggressive.
## Vilseledande marknadsföring
- mfl/sec_008.md | 8 § | 8 § | This section states that misleading marketing covered by sections 9, 10, or 12–17 is unfair if it affects or is likely to affect the recipient's ability to make an informed decision, and that the specific misleading practices listed in Annex I of Directive 2005/29/EC are always unfair.
## Reklamidentifiering
- mfl/sec_009.md | 9 § | 9 § | This provision requires that all marketing be clearly identifiable as marketing and identify the responsible party, except for content whose sole purpose is to generate attention for subsequent communications.
## Förbud mot vilseledande marknadsföring
- mfl/sec_010.md | 10 § | 10 § | This section prohibits traders from using misleading statements or omitting material information in marketing, covering specific topics such as product characteristics, pricing, business qualifications, and consumer rights.
## Begränsningar i kommunikationsmediet m.m.
- mfl/sec_011.md | 11 § | 11 § | This provision states that when assessing whether a commercial presentation is misleading, the limitations of the chosen communication medium in terms of time and space may be taken into account, along with any steps the trader has taken to provide the information through other means.
## Köperbjudanden
- mfl/sec_012.md | 12 § | 12 § | This section lists the mandatory information—such as product characteristics, pricing details, seller identity, terms, and withdrawal rights—that must accompany price offers for single or multiple products in marketing to avoid the advertisement being considered misleading.
- mfl/sec_012a.md | 12 a § | 12 a § | This section requires online marketplace providers to inform consumers whether the seller offering a priced product is a trader or not, based on the seller's own statement.
## Rankning av produkter
- mfl/sec_012b.md | 12 b § | 12 b § | This section requires businesses that let consumers search for products online to clearly state in an easily accessible part of their website or app which criteria mainly determine how results are ranked and how important those criteria are compared to others, except for search engines covered by EU rules.
## Konsumentrecensioner
- mfl/sec_012c.md | 12 c § | 12 c § | This section requires traders who provide access to consumer reviews of products to disclose whether they verify that the reviewers actually used or purchased the products and, if so, how they do it.

## Vilseledande efterbildningar
- mfl/sec_014.md | 14 § | 14 § | This section prohibits businesses from using imitations in marketing that could easily be confused with another trader's well-known and distinctive products, except when the imitation's design mainly serves a functional purpose.
