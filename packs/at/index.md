# Austria: data protection, spam and cookie laws

## Artikel 1 — (Verfassungsbestimmung)
- dsg/art_1-001.md | Art. 1 § 1 | Grundrecht auf Datenschutz | This provision establishes everyone's fundamental right to the confidentiality of their personal data, sets out when and how that right may be limited, and grants rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held in automated or manual files. | cases: ai_decision, gdpr_access, gdpr_erase, gdpr_object, gdpr_other, gdpr_rectify

### 3. Hauptstück — Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten für Zwecke der Sicherheitspolizei einschließlich des Verfassungsschutzes, des militärischen Eigenschutzes, der Aufklärung und Verfolgung von Straftaten, der Strafvollstreckung und des Maßnahmenvollzugs
### 1. Abschnitt — Allgemeine Bestimmungen
- dsg/art_2-036.md | Art. 2 § 36 | Anwendungsbereich und Begriffsbestimmungen | This provision establishes which data protection rules apply to the processing of personal data by competent authorities for criminal investigations, public safety, national security, intelligence, and military purposes, and defines the key terms used throughout those rules.
- dsg/art_2-037.md | Art. 2 § 37 | Grundsätze für die Datenverarbeitung, Kategorisierung und Datenqualität | This section establishes the principles for processing personal data, including lawfulness, purpose limitation, accuracy, and security, and sets specific rules for categorizing data subjects such as suspects, victims, and witnesses, distinguishing factual from evaluative data, and ensuring data quality when transmitting or sharing information.
- dsg/art_2-038.md | Art. 2 § 38 | Rechtmäßigkeit der Verarbeitung | This section specifies that processing personal data is lawful only when mandated by law and necessary and proportionate for a competent authority to carry out its official tasks, except when required to protect a person's vital interests.
- dsg/art_2-039.md | Art. 2 § 39 | Verarbeitung besonderer Kategorien personenbezogener Daten | This section specifies the conditions under which special categories of personal data—such as health, biometric, genetic, or information revealing racial origin, religion, political views, or sexual orientation—may be processed for the purposes listed in § 36(1), requiring strict necessity, protective safeguards, and either permission under § 38 or that the person has clearly made the data public themselves.
- dsg/art_2-040.md | Art. 2 § 40 | Verarbeitung für andere Zwecke und Übermittlung | This section sets out when personal data gathered under this chapter can be processed for a different purpose or passed on to another party, including the need for specific legal authorization, informing recipients of any special conditions, and ensuring transfers to other EU countries or institutions are not subject to stricter rules than domestic ones.
- dsg/art_2-041.md | Art. 2 § 41 | Automatisierte Entscheidungsfindung im Einzelfall | This provision governs automated individual decision-making, including profiling, by allowing it only when legally authorized, requiring protective measures for sensitive data, and banning discriminatory outcomes based on characteristics like race, religion, health, or sexual orientation.
### 2. Abschnitt — Rechte der betroffenen Person
- dsg/art_2-042.md | Art. 2 § 42 | Grundsätze | This section sets out the rules for how data controllers must communicate with individuals about their data protection rights and handle their requests, including required formats, response deadlines, when fees may be charged, identity verification, and the right to have restrictions reviewed by the data protection authority.
- dsg/art_2-043.md | Art. 2 § 43 | Information der betroffenen Person | This section outlines the information that data controllers must provide to individuals about the processing of their personal data, when that information must be given, and the specific grounds on which the additional details may be delayed or withheld.
- dsg/art_2-044.md | Art. 2 § 44 | Auskunftsrecht der betroffenen Person | This section establishes a person's right to ask a data controller whether their personal data is being processed and to receive a copy of that data along with specific details about the processing, while also setting out when this right can be restricted and how any refusal must be communicated and documented.
- dsg/art_2-045.md | Art. 2 § 45 | Recht auf Berichtigung oder Löschung personenbezogener Daten und auf Einschränkung der Verarbeitung | This section covers the rights of individuals to have inaccurate personal data corrected or incomplete data completed, to have their data erased under specific conditions, and to have processing restricted in certain situations, as well as the obligations of data controllers to notify the individual, the relevant authority, and all recipients about any rectification, erasure, or restriction.
### 3. Abschnitt — Verantwortlicher und Auftragsverarbeiter
- dsg/art_2-046.md | Art. 2 § 46 | Pflichten des Verantwortlichen | This section states that the data controller must comply with the GDPR's accountability and data protection by design obligations to ensure processing aligns with this chapter of the law.
- dsg/art_2-047.md | Art. 2 § 47 | Gemeinsam Verantwortliche | This provision states that two or more controllers who jointly determine the purposes and means of processing are joint controllers and must set out in a transparent agreement their respective responsibilities for handling data subject rights and information duties, including a designated contact point for data subjects.
- dsg/art_2-048.md | Art. 2 § 48 | Auftragsverarbeiter und Aufsicht über die Verarbeitung | This section outlines the obligations of data processors when processing personal data on behalf of a controller, including requirements for written contracts, restrictions on using sub-processors, specific duties regarding security and data subject rights, and the circumstances under which a processor is treated as a controller.
- dsg/art_2-049.md | Art. 2 § 49 | Verzeichnis von Verarbeitungstätigkeiten | This section requires data controllers and processors to maintain detailed records of their personal data processing activities, specifying required entries such as profiling usage, legal bases, international transfers, and technical security measures.
- dsg/art_2-050.md | Art. 2 § 50 | Protokollierung | This section outlines the mandatory logging of data processing activities, the specific details that must be recorded in automated and non-automated systems, and the restricted purposes for which the logs may be used.
- dsg/art_2-051.md | Art. 2 § 51 | Zusammenarbeit mit der Datenschutzbehörde | This provision requires data controllers and processors to cooperate with the data protection authority upon request when it is carrying out its tasks.
- dsg/art_2-052.md | Art. 2 § 52 | Datenschutz-Folgenabschätzung | This section requires data controllers to carry out a data protection impact assessment under the GDPR to safeguard the rights of data subjects and others affected, with the assessment documentation specifically required to show compliance with this part of the law.
- dsg/art_2-053.md | Art. 2 § 53 | Vorherige Konsultation der Datenschutzbehörde | This provision requires data controllers to consult the data protection authority before processing personal data in newly created filing systems and specifies that the authority must take certain measures within six weeks, with a possible one-month extension.
- dsg/art_2-054.md | Art. 2 § 54 | Datensicherheitsmaßnahmen | This section requires data controllers and processors to implement technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the risk of processing personal data, and lists ten specific controls for automated processing systems.
- dsg/art_2-055.md | Art. 2 § 55 | Meldung von Verletzungen an die Datenschutzbehörde | This section requires data controllers to report personal data breaches to the data protection authority and to promptly share breach details with controllers in other EU member states when their data is affected.
- dsg/art_2-056.md | Art. 2 § 56 | Benachrichtigung der betroffenen Person von Verletzungen | This section requires data controllers to notify affected individuals about personal data breaches in accordance with GDPR Article 34, and allows that notification to be postponed, restricted, or omitted under the conditions set out in § 43(4).
- dsg/art_2-057.md | Art. 2 § 57 | Benennung, Stellung und Aufgaben des Datenschutzbeauftragten | This section covers the appointment, legal status, and tasks of the data protection officer, as well as the requirement to publish their contact details and notify the data protection authority.

- tkg/sec_023.md | § 23 | Wettbewerb | This provision requires the regulatory authority to promote effective competition and prevent market distortions when granting, modifying, or renewing radio frequency assignments, and details the specific measures it may take—such as capping spectrum holdings, imposing access or roaming conditions, reserving spectrum for new entrants, or restricting transfers—along with the procedural rules and forward-looking market analysis guiding those decisions.
- tkg/sec_024.md | § 24 | Frequenznutzungsentgelt | This section sets out the rules for the frequency usage fee that holders of competitively allocated radio frequencies must pay, including how the minimum bid is set, when it can be adjusted based on market value, how the fee is specified in the allocation decision, and the deadline for payment.

- tkg/sec_074.md | § 74 | Ausübung von Rechten | This section requires that rights under sections 51 to 70 be exercised in the least intrusive way possible, with maximum care for the affected properties, facilities, and third-party rights, while ensuring continued proper use during any works and prompt restoration of the site to a proper condition afterward.

## 10. Abschnitt — Adressierung und Notrufe
- tkg/sec_111.md | § 111 | Ziele | This section states that the aim is to efficiently structure and manage all communication parameters in order to meet the requirements of end users, providers, and operators in an open, objective, transparent, proportionate, and non-discriminatory way.
- tkg/sec_112.md | § 112 | Plan für Kommunikationsparameter | This section requires the regulatory authority to issue an ordinance establishing a plan for communication parameters—including emergency numbers such as 112 and 116000—that sets conditions for their allocation, rules for their use and transfer, and deadlines for updating existing non-compliant numbers, while obliging providers and operators to cooperate in its implementation.
- tkg/sec_113.md | § 113 | Planänderungen | This section sets out the rules for when the regulator can change telecommunications plans, how affected license holders must be notified, the safeguards for existing rights and costs, and the requirement that license holders pay for and implement the changes themselves.
- tkg/sec_114.md | § 114 | Zuständigkeit zur Zuteilung von Kommunikationsparametern, Verfahren | This section sets out the regulatory authority's role in allocating and managing communication parameters such as telephone numbers, including the application process, conditions attached to allocations, and the rules for transferring or passing on usage rights.
- tkg/sec_115.md | § 115 | Nutzung | This section states that allocation of communication parameters grants only a right to use them rather than any right to specific parameters, and that using parameters without a proper right of use is not permitted.
- tkg/sec_116.md | § 116 | Nutzungsentgelte | This section requires a usage fee for every communication parameter, allows the responsible federal minister to set the fee amount by regulation based on costs, economic benefit, and optimal use, and applies the same fee obligation to parameters already in use or held when the law takes effect.
- tkg/sec_117.md | § 117 | Erlöschen der Zuteilung | This section lists the ways a telecommunications allocation can end—such as expiry, waiver, revocation, or the holder's death—and specifies the grounds for revocation and the written procedure for waiver.
- tkg/sec_118.md | § 118 | Wechsel des Anbieters von Internetzugangsdiensten | This section sets out the rules for switching between internet access service providers on the same infrastructure, covering information duties, cooperation between providers, limits on service interruption, and the requirement for the end user's explicit consent.
- tkg/sec_119.md | § 119 | Nummernübertragbarkeit | This section sets out the rules for number portability, requiring telecom providers to let customers switch operators while keeping their existing phone number, and covering the transfer process, timing limits, service continuity, return of numbers not ported after a contract ends, and the regulator's power to set technical details and compensation rules.
- tkg/sec_120.md | § 120 | Entgeltansprüche bei der Nummernübertragung | This section covers the rules on fees for transferring a phone number to another provider, including that the end user cannot be charged for the transfer itself and that prepaid providers must refund any remaining credit, with any fee for that refund only permitted if specified in the contract and proportionate to the actual costs incurred.
- tkg/sec_121.md | § 121 | Maßnahmen gegen Nummernmissbrauch | This section establishes the regulatory authority as a reporting point for phone number misuse and grants it powers to order measures such as blocking numbers or prohibiting charges when significant misuse occurs.
- tkg/sec_122.md | § 122 | Notrufe | This section requires telecommunications providers and network operators to ensure free, uninterrupted access to the 112 emergency number and other emergency services—including text-based calls, caller identification, and location data—and to guarantee equal access for people with disabilities and roaming users.
- tkg/sec_123.md | § 123 | Ausfallsicherheit | This section requires telecommunications providers, payphone operators, and network operators to maintain backup equipment ensuring emergency calls connect during network outages, authorizes the regulator to set technical standards for such resilience, mandates annual functionality checks and reporting, and requires immediate notification of security incidents significantly affecting emergency number reachability.
- tkg/sec_124.md | § 124 | Auskünfte an Betreiber von Notdiensten | This section requires telecom providers to immediately give emergency services caller location and subscriber data during emergencies—including from unregistered mobile devices—and sets rules for technical interfaces, documentation, later notification to users, and regulatory technical standards.
- tkg/sec_125.md | § 125 | Öffentliches Warnsystem | This section requires telecommunications providers to transmit text-based public emergency warnings to end users on behalf of competent government authorities during major emergencies or disasters, and sets out rules on technical implementation, free reception, data processing, public logging by the regulator, and reimbursement of compliance costs.
- tkg/sec_126.md | § 126 | Nutzerverzeichnis und Auskunftsdienst | This section sets out rules for providers of number-based interpersonal communication services on maintaining user directories, operating telephone directory-assistance services, sharing directory data with other providers and publishers, and withholding user data from third parties when a user opts out of listing.
## 11. Abschnitt — Schutz der Nutzer
- tkg/sec_127.md | § 127 | Ausnahmen vom Anwendungsbereich dieses Abschnittes | This provision exempts micro-enterprises that offer only number-independent interpersonal communication services from most of the chapter's rules (except § 128) and requires them to give end users verifiable notice of this exemption before entering into a contract.
- tkg/sec_128.md | § 128 | Nichtdiskriminierung | This section states that everyone is entitled to use communication services under published terms and prices and prohibits providers from applying different access or usage conditions based on a user's nationality, residence, or place of establishment unless such differences are objectively justified.
- tkg/sec_129.md | § 129 | Informationspflichten für Verträge | This section requires telecom providers to give consumers (and small businesses or non-profits unless they waive the right) specific pre-contractual information and a standardized contract summary before a contract is concluded, and sets out the required format, timing, and legal effect of that information.
- tkg/sec_130.md | § 130 | Kostenbeschränkung | This section requires providers of internet and communication services to offer consumers tools to monitor and control usage, and allows the regulator to set spending caps and mandate additional safeguards such as warnings or service blocks to prevent unexpectedly high bills.
- tkg/sec_131.md | § 131 | Entgelte und Regulierung von Diensten Dritter | This section empowers the telecommunications regulator to issue ordinances setting rules on charges, billing transparency, and consumer protection for third-party services provided via special number ranges, and requires the regulator to maintain and publish a directory of the providers of those number-bound services.
- tkg/sec_132.md | § 132 | Transparenz und Veröffentlichung von Informationen | This section sets out the transparency and publication duties of telecom providers, requiring them to make their general terms and conditions and pricing information clear, comprehensive, machine-readable and accessible, and listing the specific service, quality, contract, fee, security, emergency-access and data-protection details those documents must contain.
- tkg/sec_133.md | § 133 | Widerspruchsverfahren und Verfahrensvorschriften | This section sets out the rules requiring telecom providers to notify the regulatory authority of their general terms and conditions and pricing before offering services, including exemptions, notice periods for changes, and the authority's right to object within six weeks if the terms violate the law.
- tkg/sec_134.md | § 134 | Tarif- und Angebotsvergleich | This section requires the telecommunications regulator to offer a free, independent tool for end users to compare prices and service quality of internet and communication services, sets mandatory standards for such comparison tools, and permits third parties to use published provider data to create their own comparators.
- tkg/sec_135.md | § 135 | Vertragslaufzeit und –kündigung | This section regulates contract terms for telecom services provided to consumers and small businesses, covering maximum contract lengths, termination rights and fees, mandatory customer notifications about contract endings and tariff options, and rules for continuing service when customers relocate.
- tkg/sec_136.md | § 136 | Bündelprodukte | This section covers the rules for bundled service packages containing internet access or number-based communication services, including extended consumer protections for all bundle components, the right to terminate the entire bundle if individual parts are breached, prohibitions on extending contract terms when adding extra services, and application to small businesses and non-profits unless waived.
- tkg/sec_137.md | § 137 | Eintragung in das Nutzerverzeichnis | This section covers users' rights to be listed in, review, correct, or delete their entries from publicly accessible subscriber directories, including the right to have specific data included free of charge and the right to opt out of listing entirely without charge, along with the requirement that providers inform users of these rights.
- tkg/sec_138.md | § 138 | Rechnung und Einzelentgeltnachweis | This section governs how telecom providers must issue bills and itemized connection statements for internet access and number-based communication services, covering the required details and chronological listing, the option to receive paper copies, electronic delivery and seven-year retention rules, limits on displaying dialed numbers and excluding free or emergency calls, and the deadlines for deleting the underlying data.
- tkg/sec_139.md | § 139 | Anzeige der Nummer des Anrufers | This section covers the rules for caller ID display and suppression in voice communication services, including the free options for callers and recipients to block or reject number display, its application to international calls, and the regulator's enforcement powers.
- tkg/sec_140.md | § 140 | Automatische Anrufweiterschaltung | Network operators must disable third-party-initiated automatic call forwarding to a user's device upon request and free of charge, cooperating with other operators when multiple are involved in the forwarding.
- tkg/sec_141.md | § 141 | Fangschaltung, belästigende Anrufe | This section defines caller ID tracing (Fangschaltung) and requires providers to implement it or unmask suppressed numbers when an end user wants to track harassing calls, allows a reasonable fee, and mandates that the operator store and share the results for calls the user credibly reports as harassing.
- tkg/sec_142.md | § 142 | Dienstesperren | This section requires telecom providers to offer end users a free annual block on chargeable third-party services (including premium-rate numbers over €0.20 per minute or per event) and allows providers to permanently block access if the user disputes charges in two consecutive billing periods.
- tkg/sec_143.md | § 143 | Zahlungsverzug | This section specifies when and how telecommunications providers may suspend or disconnect services due to a customer's failure to pay, covering required warnings and deadlines, permissible fees, the ban on blocking emergency calls, and special protections for universal services.
- tkg/sec_144.md | § 144 | Weiterleitung von E-Mails | This section states that when an internet access contract ends, the end user can demand that the provider continue forwarding emails to their provider-branded email address free of charge for twelve months, and that the regulatory authority may issue regulations specifying the technical details of how this forwarding process should work.
- tkg/sec_145.md | § 145 | Überprüfung der Entgelte | This section outlines the procedure for end users to challenge billed telecommunications charges, including the provider's obligation to verify billing factors, the deferral of payment deadlines during dispute resolution, refund and interest rules, and the use of flat-rate compensation when the exact correct amount cannot be calculated.
## 12. Abschnitt — Verwendung von Amateurfunkstellen
- tkg/sec_146.md | § 146 | Berechtigungsumfang | This section outlines the scope of an amateur radio license, covering permitted station types and locations, transmission restrictions, and special rights for Class 1 holders.
- tkg/sec_147.md | § 147 | Nachrichteninhalt | This section regulates the content of amateur radio messages in Austria, restricting them to technical tests, operational information, or trivial personal remarks and requiring that all communications occur only between licensed stations.
- tkg/sec_148.md | § 148 | Not- und Katastrophenfunkverkehr | This section defines emergency and disaster radio communications, outlines amateur radio operators' obligations to assist authorities during emergencies, and specifies procedures for emergency calls and drills.
- tkg/sec_149.md | § 149 | Rufzeichen | This section covers the requirements for transmitting assigned call signs at the start, end, and during radio communications, as well as the rules for club stations using their own or a co-user's call sign.
- tkg/sec_150.md | § 150 | Rufzeichenliste | This section sets out the rules for publishing a call sign list that includes amateur radio operators' names, station locations, call signs, and license classes, requires consent for personal data, and limits use of the data to amateur radio purposes only.
- tkg/sec_151.md | § 151 | Mitbenützung | This section sets out when amateur radio license holders may allow others who have passed the amateur radio exam to use their station, the limits on such use based on the user's exam category and the station's license, possible exceptions for training purposes, and the license holder's continued responsibility for legal compliance and supervision.
- tkg/sec_152.md | § 152 | Funktagebuch | This section specifies when a radio logbook must be maintained—during emergency or disaster radio traffic and exercises, or upon request by the telecommunications authority—and what must be recorded in it, including essential transmission details and, for emergency and disaster communications, the complete content with the identity and location of the other station.
- tkg/sec_153.md | § 153 | Sicherungsmaßnahmen | This section requires the holder of an amateur radio station to take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized persons from putting the station into operation.
## 13. Abschnitt — Amateurfunkprüfungszeugnisse
- tkg/sec_154.md | § 154 | Voraussetzungen für die Ausstellung | This section states that an amateur radio examination certificate is issued upon application to applicants who demonstrate their professional qualification by successfully passing the amateur radio examination.
- tkg/sec_155.md | § 155 | Antrag auf Ausstellung | This section requires that applications for an amateur radio examination certificate be submitted in writing to the telecommunications office and include the applicant's name, address, and the desired examination category.
- tkg/sec_156.md | § 156 | Zurückziehung des Antrages | This provision states that an application for an amateur radio examination certificate is considered withdrawn if the applicant misses the scheduled exam without proving no fault, withdraws during the exam, or fails the exam.
- tkg/sec_157.md | § 157 | Gegenstände der Prüfung, Ergänzungsprüfung | This section sets out the subjects covered by the amateur radio exam, allows the written parts to be automated or multiple-choice, empowers the minister to define exam categories and procedures by regulation, and permits holders of a lower-category certificate to sit a supplementary exam for a higher category.
- tkg/sec_158.md | § 158 | Einrichtung einer Prüfungskommission | This section establishes an examination commission at the telecommunications office, consisting of two members appointed by the minister for five years—a legal expert from the authority who chairs the commission and an experienced amateur radio operator for operations and technology—and provides that the chair has the deciding vote in the event of a tie.
- tkg/sec_159.md | § 159 | Anerkennung ausländischer Zeugnisse | This section enables the Federal Minister for Agriculture, Regions and Tourism to recognize foreign certificates by ordinance, provided that reciprocity exists and the foreign training is equivalent.
## 14. Abschnitt — Kommunikationsgeheimnis, Datenschutz
- tkg/sec_160.md | § 160 | Allgemeines | This section defines the scope of the rules for processing personal and non-public data in public communications services and networks, and provides definitions for terms such as provider, user, traffic data, location data, and personal data breach.
- tkg/sec_161.md | § 161 | Kommunikationsgeheimnis | This section defines the secrecy of communications as covering content, traffic, and location data (including unsuccessful connection attempts) and sets out who must keep such data confidential and when interception or disclosure is prohibited or permitted.
- tkg/sec_162.md | § 162 | Technische Einrichtungen | This section sets out providers' duties to equip their networks for lawful communication surveillance and data requests, the rules for compensating 80% of the equipment costs, and the technical requirements for secure data transmission.
- tkg/sec_163.md | § 163 | Datensicherheitsmaßnahmen | This section sets out the data security duties of public communication service operators, including GDPR-aligned security measures, user notifications for special confidentiality risks, and regulatory oversight.
- tkg/sec_164.md | § 164 | Sicherheitsverletzungen | This section requires operators of public communication services to report security breaches involving personal or non-public data to the data protection authority and, where applicable, to affected individuals, and specifies related documentation, exceptions, and reporting duties.
- tkg/sec_165.md | § 165 | Datenschutz – Allgemeines | This section limits telecom operators' processing of master, traffic, location and content data to what the communication service requires, requires revocable consent for marketing or value-added services, obliges providers to tell users which personal data they process, on what legal basis and for how long, and allows storing or accessing information on a user's terminal equipment only with the user's consent, except where this is needed solely to transmit a message or to provide a service the user explicitly asked for. | cases: cookies, eprivacy
- tkg/sec_166.md | § 166 | Stammdaten | This section sets out the rules for how telecommunications providers may process customer master data, including the permitted purposes such as contract management, billing, directories, and emergency services, the mandatory identity verification required before signing a contract or recharging, and the obligation to delete this data after the contractual relationship ends.
- tkg/sec_167.md | § 167 | Verkehrsdaten | This section sets out the rules for when telecommunications providers may store, process, delete, or disclose traffic data, including requirements for billing, limits on internal use, and permitted disclosures to courts, prosecutors, and security authorities.
- tkg/sec_168.md | § 168 | Inhaltsdaten | This section covers the general prohibition on storing communication content data, permitting only legally authorized or technically necessary short-term storage and requiring immediate deletion once the data is no longer needed.
- tkg/sec_169.md | § 169 | Andere Standortdaten als Verkehrsdaten | This section governs when and how location data other than traffic data may be processed, requiring anonymization or revocable user consent and limiting use to what is strictly necessary for the service.
- tkg/sec_170.md | § 170 | Datensicherheit bei der Übermittlung von betriebsnotwendigen Verkehrs- und Standortdaten zu Auskunftszwecken an gesetzlich berechtigte Behörden | This section sets out the required data-security measures for transmitting operationally necessary traffic and location data to legally authorized authorities, including a central transit point, transport encryption, end-to-end content encryption, and advanced electronic signatures.
- tkg/sec_171.md | § 171 | Durchlaufstelle – Grundstruktur | This section sets out the basic structure of the secure electronic clearinghouse through which telecommunications providers must handle certain data inquiries, specifying encryption and authentication requirements, preventing the data processor from accessing personal content, requiring audit-proof statistical recording, listing exceptions for urgent security cases, and authorizing detailed technical regulations by ordinance.
- tkg/sec_172.md | § 172 | Einrichtung und Betrieb der Durchlaufstelle – Auftraggeber und Durchführung | This section assigns responsibility for the transit point's setup, operation, certificate management and data security to the Federal Minister for Agriculture, Regions and Tourism, delegates the actual execution to the Federal Computing Centre GmbH as a data processor, and allows the Minister to engage a service provider to audit the technical implementation.
- tkg/sec_173.md | § 173 | Nutzerverzeichnis | This section restricts how telecommunications operators may process, use, and share data from subscriber directories, limiting such use to purposes of number-based communication services and prohibiting profiling or categorization, while permitting certain disclosures subject to the same limits.
- tkg/sec_174.md | § 174 | Unerbetene Nachrichten | This section covers the prohibition of unsolicited advertising calls, faxes, emails, and text messages without prior consent, including exceptions for existing customers, caller identification rules, and where offenses committed abroad are treated as occurring. | cases: eprivacy
## 15. Abschnitt — Aufsichtsrechte und Transparenz
- tkg/sec_175.md | § 175 | Umfang | This section outlines the authorities responsible for supervising communication services and telecommunications installations, defines what counts as a telecommunications installation, and details their powers to inspect equipment, enter premises, and demand information and documents from operators.
- tkg/sec_176.md | § 176 | Durchsuchung | This section authorizes telecommunications authorities to order or, in urgent cases, conduct searches of property, homes, persons, and vehicles when an unauthorized radio transmitter endangers people or property or international obligations require it, prescribes how such searches must be carried out with proportionality and care, and requires a brief written record to be given or left with the person searched.
- tkg/sec_177.md | § 177 | Aufsichtsmaßnahmen | This section outlines the telecommunications authority's powers to order measures against interference, shut down unauthorized installations, permit disconnection of harmful certified equipment, and allow emergency disconnections by network operators under specific conditions.
- tkg/sec_178.md | § 178 | Einstellung des Betriebes | This provision authorizes the Austrian federal minister to suspend or restrict the operation of telecommunications facilities and electronic communications networks for reasons of public order or grounds permitted under EU treaty law, while requiring due justification, notification to the European Commission, and stating that no right to compensation arises from such orders.
- tkg/sec_179.md | § 179 | Kontrollgeräte im Amateurfunk | This section requires amateur radio stations to be operated so as not to interfere with other telecommunications systems and empowers the minister to specify by regulation which amateur stations must have monitoring equipment to check technical compliance during operation.
- tkg/sec_180.md | § 180 | Sperre von Mehrwertdienstenummern | This section empowers the regulatory authority to order the immediate blocking of premium rate numbers when there are justified suspicions of violations of pricing information or proper use rules that could cause significant economic harm to users, and requires that blocking orders be published on the authority's website along with a maintained list of blocked numbers.
- tkg/sec_181.md | § 181 | Informationspflichten | This section sets out the duties of telecom operators, providers, and spectrum holders to hand over information to regulators and law enforcement for compliance, market statistics, and criminal investigations, and mandates record-keeping of cell tower locations and notification of low-power wireless access points.
- tkg/sec_182.md | § 182 | Transparenz | This section requires the regulatory authority to publish decisions of fundamental importance, market data, and other information that supports an open competitive market, while protecting personal data and business secrets.
- tkg/sec_183.md | § 183 | Information durch die Regulierungsbehörde | This section requires the regulatory authority to share information with the European Commission and with BEREC or other national regulators upon their justified written request, and to notify operators when their data is sent to the Commission.
- tkg/sec_184.md | § 184 | Aufsichtsverfahren | This section sets out the supervisory procedure the regulator follows when it has indications that a company is violating telecommunications law, including notifying the company and giving it a chance to respond or fix the issue, ordering corrective measures if the issue persists, suspending or banning the company's operations for gross or repeated violations, taking emergency action in cases of immediate danger, and defining who qualifies as a party in the proceedings.
- tkg/sec_185.md | § 185 | Regulierungskonzept; Evaluierung von Verordnungen | This section requires regulatory authorities to help maintain a uniform regulatory concept and obliges the regulatory authority to review its own ordinances and the practical effectiveness of contract summaries at least every three years, publishing the results on its website.
- tkg/sec_186.md | § 186 | Beobachtung des Wettbewerbsgeschehens | This section authorizes the regulatory authority to monitor competition in the electronic communications sector using publicly available or provided data and to publish related information or studies.
## 16. Abschnitt — Strafbestimmungen
- tkg/sec_187.md | § 187 | Verletzung von Rechten der Benützer | This provision makes it a criminal offense, punishable by up to three months' imprisonment or a fine of up to 180 daily rates, for persons bound by telecommunications secrecy to unlawfully disclose the fact or content of communications or to falsify, alter, suppress, or withhold messages, and it requires that prosecution be initiated only upon the victim's complaint.
- tkg/sec_188.md | § 188 | Verwaltungsstrafbestimmungen | This section lists the administrative fines for telecommunications violations, ranging from €1,000 to €100,000 with possible imprisonment of up to six weeks for uncollectible fines, covering offenses such as unauthorized radio operation, amateur radio rule breaches, emergency call failures, and data protection violations.
- tkg/sec_189.md | § 189 | Veröffentlichung des Straferkenntnisses | This section allows courts to order the publication of a criminal conviction in periodicals at the convicted person's expense when the offender has two prior convictions for similar offenses and is likely to reoffend.
- tkg/sec_190.md | § 190 | Abschöpfung der Bereicherung | This section allows the telecommunications regulatory authority to request that the Cartel Court order a company to disgorge economic advantages gained from violating telecom laws, up to 10% of the company's previous year's turnover, with the funds going to finance the regulatory body.
## 17. Abschnitt — Behörden und Verfahrensbestimmungen
- tkg/sec_191.md | § 191 | Fernmeldebehörden | This section identifies the Federal Minister for Agriculture, Regions and Tourism and the Telecommunications Office subordinate to her as the telecommunications authorities.
- tkg/sec_192.md | § 192 | Zuständigkeit | This section sets out which Austrian authorities are competent to carry out official acts under the Telecommunications Act 2021, covering the territorial scope of the telecom authorities, the default competence of the Telecommunications Office, specific powers of the responsible federal minister, appeals to the Federal Administrative Court, and automated issuance of official decisions.
- tkg/sec_193.md | § 193 | Mitwirkung durch Organe des öffentlichen Sicherheitsdienstes, Vollstreckung | This section sets out the duty of public security organs to assist the telecommunications office in carrying out surveillance measures and states that telecommunications authorities enforce their own non-monetary orders under administrative enforcement law.
- tkg/sec_194.md | § 194 | Aufgaben der Rundfunk und Telekom Regulierungs-GmbH | This section assigns the general regulatory duties under the Telecommunications Act to RTR GmbH, designates it as the national regulatory authority under certain EU regulations, and grants independence to its managing director for telecommunications and postal matters in specific cases.
- tkg/sec_194a.md | § 194a | Aufgaben der Servicestelle für Künstliche Intelligenz | This section defines the duties of the Artificial Intelligence Service Center within the telecommunications regulator, covering public advisory services on AI regulation, technical documentation, knowledge exchange, cybersecurity, and high-risk AI systems, plus coordination roles and required consultation with the media division on media-related AI issues.
- tkg/sec_195.md | § 195 | Telekom-Control-Kommission | This section establishes the Telekom-Control-Kommission to perform the tasks in § 198, locates it at the Rundfunk und Telekom Regulierungs-GmbH, and confirms that its members are independent and not bound by instructions.
- tkg/sec_196.md | § 196 | Zusammensetzung der Telekom-Control-Kommission | This section sets out how the three-member Telekom-Control-Kommission is appointed and composed, who is barred from serving, how substitutes and vacancies are handled, and what remuneration members receive.
- tkg/sec_197.md | § 197 | Vorsitzender und Geschäftsordnung | This section provides that the judicial member chairs the Telekom-Control Commission, that the Commission adopts its own rules of procedure and entrusts one member with managing ongoing affairs, and that valid decisions require unanimity with no abstentions, permitting deliberation and voting by circulation or telecommunications.
- tkg/sec_198.md | § 198 | Aufgaben | This section enumerates the duties of Austria's Telekom-Control-Kommission, ranging from frequency allocation and market regulation to universal service funding and cross-border dispute resolution.
- tkg/sec_199.md | § 199 | Zuständigkeit der KommAustria | This section specifies that KommAustria, rather than the general telecommunications regulator, handles regulatory matters involving electronic audio and audiovisual media, broadcasting, additional services, and video-sharing platforms, and lists the specific powers it exercises in those cases along with rules for overlapping jurisdiction and appeals.
- tkg/sec_200.md | § 200 | Verfahrensvorschriften, Instanzenzug | This section sets out the procedural rules for proceedings before the Austrian telecom regulators, covering dispute-resolution referrals to the RTR-GmbH, mandatory electronic filing, domestic service addresses for non-EEA providers, decision deadlines and cooperation duties, a ban on new evidence after the investigation closes, and appeals to the Federal Administrative Court.
- tkg/sec_201.md | § 201 | Verfahren vor dem Bundesverwaltungsgericht | This section sets out the rules for appeals to the Federal Administrative Court against decisions of telecommunications regulatory authorities, including that appeals do not automatically suspend the decision unless the court grants a stay to prevent severe and irreparable harm, and that cases involving the Telekom-Control-Kommission are decided by panels.
- tkg/sec_202.md | § 202 | Großverfahren | This section sets out the special rules for large-scale proceedings involving more than 100 participants, including public announcement by edict on the authority's website, a six-week deadline for individuals to establish their party status, and the use of electronic communication for notifications and file access.
- tkg/sec_203.md | § 203 | Beilegung von Streitigkeiten zwischen Unternehmen | This section sets out the process for referring disputes between telecom operators or providers over access agreements or related obligations to the regulatory authority when negotiations fail within six weeks, including the authority's ability to initiate proceedings on its own.
- tkg/sec_204.md | § 204 | Beilegung grenzüberschreitender Streitigkeiten | This section sets out the rules for national regulatory authorities in different EU member states to coordinate and, where trade between member states is affected, refer cross-border disputes concerning the scope of Directive (EU) 2018/1972 (excluding radio frequency coordination) to BEREC for an opinion before taking action.
- tkg/sec_205.md | § 205 | Schlichtungsverfahren | This section describes the conciliation procedure through which end users, providers, interest groups, and funding bodies can bring disputes or complaints—covering service quality, universal service, alleged legal violations, and payment issues—to the regulatory authority, which must attempt an amicable settlement or communicate its view, while outlining the parties' duties to cooperate and the authority's ability to issue procedural guidelines.
- tkg/sec_206.md | § 206 | Konsultationsverfahren | This section outlines the consultation procedures requiring Austrian authorities to solicit and publish comments from interested parties on draft telecommunications measures with significant market impact, including minimum 30-day comment periods, suspended deadlines, notification to the Radio Spectrum Policy Group regarding harmonized frequency use, and separate consultation on end-user and consumer rights.
- tkg/sec_207.md | § 207 | Koordinationsverfahren | This section describes the coordination procedure for draft regulatory measures that affect trade between EU member states, including notification to the European Commission, BEREC, and other national regulators, mandatory waiting periods, and the handling of objections or withdrawal requests.
- tkg/sec_208.md | § 208 | Behandlung von Geschäftsgeheimnissen | This section outlines the rules for how the regulatory authority must protect trade secrets, determine what qualifies as one by balancing competing interests, and handle doubts about whether secrecy is justified.
- tkg/sec_209.md | § 209 | Zusammenarbeit mit anderen Behörden | This section explains how Austrian regulatory bodies, competition and data protection authorities, statistical and surveying agencies, and other ministries share information, enter cooperation agreements, and provide data or assistance to support enforcement of the Telecommunications Act.
- tkg/sec_210.md | § 210 | Antragsrechte beim Kartellgericht | This section sets out when the telecommunications regulatory authority must examine suspected cartel law violations and file applications with the Cartel Court, including a mandatory duty to apply when cartel prohibitions are breached and the purposes of the Telecommunications Act are affected.
## 18. Abschnitt — Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen
- tkg/sec_211.md | § 211 | Außerkrafttreten von Rechtsvorschriften | This section states that the Telecommunications Act 2003 is repealed when the 2021 Act comes into force.
- tkg/sec_212.md | § 212 | Übergangsbestimmungen | This section sets out the transitional rules for moving from the previous telecommunications law to the 2021 Act, covering pending proceedings, existing licenses and agreements, amateur radio authorizations, and deadlines for new obligations.
- tkg/sec_213.md | § 213 | Verweisungen | This section states that references in this law to other federal laws are to be read as references to the version of those laws currently in force.
- tkg/sec_214.md | § 214 | Verlautbarungen | This section sets out where regulations and official announcements from the Federal Minister and the regulatory authority must be published, including the Federal Law Gazette II and the authority's website, and how technical documents may be made available for inspection during office hours.
