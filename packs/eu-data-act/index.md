# Data Act

## Recitals
- rec_001-010.md | Recitals 1–10 | Preamble | This part introduces the purpose and scope of the Data Act, explaining its goals for data sharing, barriers to overcome, and how it balances rights while protecting personal data.
- rec_011-020.md | Recitals 11–20 | Preamble | This part explains the scope and key definitions of the Data Act, focusing on connected products, related services, and data accessibility requirements.
- rec_021-030.md | Recitals 21–30 | Preamble | This part covers rules on data access, sharing, and user rights for connected products, including multi-user scenarios, virtual assistants, and transparency requirements before purchase.
- rec_031-040.md | Recitals 31–40 | Preamble | This part covers trade secret protection when data must be shared, the limits on using shared data to develop competing products, the duties of third parties receiving data at the user's request (personal data rules, terminal equipment consent, no dark patterns, data minimisation, no profiling), and the exclusion of designated gatekeepers as data recipients.
- rec_041-050.md | Recitals 41–50 | Preamble | This part explains the rules for data sharing obligations, compensation, and exceptions for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises under the Data Act.
- rec_051-060.md | Recitals 51–60 | Preamble | This part covers transparency in compensation for data sharing, dispute resolution procedures, and rules on unfair contractual terms related to data access and use.
- rec_061-070.md | Recitals 61–70 | Preamble | This part covers the criteria and lists of unfair contractual terms, and the rules for public sector bodies, the Commission, the European Central Bank and Union bodies to request data from businesses in cases of exceptional need such as public emergencies.
- rec_071-080.md | Recitals 71–80 | Preamble | This part covers how data holders may decline or modify requests from public bodies, the use of non-personal or anonymised data, the reuse, sharing and compensation of data provided to public bodies, cross-border requests, and the purpose and scope of switching between data processing services such as cloud services.
- rec_081-090.md | Recitals 81–90 | Preamble | This part explains the types of data processing services, the process and requirements for switching between providers, and the rules around charges and interoperability.
- rec_091-100.md | Recitals 91–100 | Preamble | This part covers the obligations and requirements for providers of data processing services to support switching, interoperability, and multi-cloud strategies, including exemptions and the role of standards.
- rec_101-110.md | Recitals 101–110 | Preamble | This part covers the rules on safeguards against third-country access to non-personal data, interoperability standards, smart contracts, enforcement mechanisms, and the roles of competent authorities and the European Data Innovation Board.
- rec_111-119.md | Recitals 111–119 | Preamble | This part covers the Commission’s role in developing model contractual terms, clarifications on database rights, delegated and implementing powers, sector-specific rules, competition law, timelines for implementation, and the legal basis for the Regulation.
## CHAPTER I — GENERAL PROVISIONS
- art_001.md | Article 1 | Subject matter and scope | This part defines the purpose, scope, and key obligations of the Data Act regarding data sharing, access, and interoperability for connected products and services.
- art_002.md | Article 2 | Definitions | This part defines key terms used throughout the Data Act to ensure clarity and consistent interpretation of the regulation.
## CHAPTER II — BUSINESS TO CONSUMER AND BUSINESS TO BUSINESS DATA SHARING
- art_003.md | Article 3 | Obligation to make product data and related service data accessible to the user | This part explains the legal requirements for making product and service data accessible to users of connected products.
- art_004.md | Article 4 | The rights and obligations of users and data holders with regard to access, use and making available product data and related service data | This part covers the rights and obligations of users and data holders regarding access, use, and sharing of product and service data, including trade secrets, personal data, and restrictions on competitive use.
- art_005.md | Article 5 | Right of the user to share data with third parties | This part explains the user's right to share their data with third parties, including conditions, restrictions, and protections for trade secrets and personal data.
- art_006.md | Article 6 | Obligations of third parties receiving data at the request of the user | This section outlines the obligations of third parties when handling data shared by users under the Data Act, including restrictions on usage, sharing, and competitive practices.
- art_007.md | Article 7 | Scope of business-to-consumer and business-to-business data sharing obligations | This part explains which small and medium-sized businesses are exempt from data-sharing obligations under the Data Act.
## CHAPTER III — OBLIGATIONS FOR DATA HOLDERS OBLIGED TO MAKE DATA AVAILABLE PURSUANT TO UNION LAW
- art_008.md | Article 8 | Conditions under which data holders make data available to data recipients | This part outlines the rules for when and how data holders must share data with others in business-to-business situations, including fairness, transparency, and non-discrimination requirements.
- art_009.md | Article 9 | Compensation for making data available | This part covers the rules for determining fair and reasonable compensation when making data available in business-to-business transactions, including specific considerations for SMEs and research organizations.
- art_010.md | Article 10 | Dispute settlement | This part explains how disputes related to data sharing and processing services are resolved through certified bodies, including rules on fees, procedures, and timelines.
- art_011.md | Article 11 | Technical protection measures on the unauthorised use or disclosure of data | This part explains the rules for using technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access or disclosure and the consequences for violating those protections.
- art_012.md | Article 12 | Scope of obligations for data holders obliged pursuant to Union law to make data available | This part explains when and how obligations apply for businesses that must share data under EU or national laws in business-to-business relationships.
## CHAPTER IV — UNFAIR CONTRACTUAL TERMS RELATED TO DATA ACCESS AND USE BETWEEN ENTERPRISES
- art_013.md | Article 13 | Unfair contractual terms unilaterally imposed on another enterprise | This part explains when contractual terms about data access or liability are unfair if one business imposes them on another without negotiation.
## CHAPTER V — MAKING DATA AVAILABLE TO PUBLIC SECTOR BODIES, THE COMMISSION, THE EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK AND UNION BODIES ON THE BASIS OF AN EXCEPTIONAL NEED
- art_014.md | Article 14 | Obligation to make data available on the basis of an exceptional need | This part explains when private companies must share data with public bodies if there is a strong, justified need for that data to fulfill official duties.
- art_015.md | Article 15 | Exceptional need to use data | This part explains when public bodies can access data in exceptional circumstances, including during emergencies or when required by law.
- art_016.md | Article 16 | Relationship with other obligations to make data available to public sector bodies, the Commission, the European Central Bank and Union bodies | This section clarifies how Article 16 interacts with other legal obligations for data sharing with public bodies and law enforcement.
- art_017.md | Article 17 | Requests for data to be made available | This part outlines the requirements and procedures for public sector bodies and other entities to request data under the Data Act, including what must be included in such requests and how they should be handled.
- art_018.md | Article 18 | Compliance with requests for data | This part explains how data holders must respond to requests for data access, including when they can refuse or modify such requests.
- art_019.md | Article 19 | Obligations of public sector bodies, the Commission, the European Central Bank and Union bodies | This part outlines the legal obligations for public sector bodies and EU institutions when handling requested data, including usage restrictions, security measures, and trade secret protections.
- art_020.md | Article 20 | Compensation in cases of an exceptional need | This part covers the rules for compensation when data holders provide data in cases of exceptional need, including public emergencies and specific public interest tasks.
- art_021.md | Article 21 | Sharing of data obtained in the context of an exceptional need with research organisations or statistical bodies | This part explains when and how public bodies can share data for research or official statistics, including rules for recipients and data holders.
- art_022.md | Article 22 | Mutual assistance and cross-border cooperation | This part explains how public bodies and EU institutions must cooperate and assist each other to enforce the rules, including the process for requesting data across EU borders.
## CHAPTER VI — SWITCHING BETWEEN DATA PROCESSING SERVICES
- art_023.md | Article 23 | Removing obstacles to effective switching | This part explains the rules for data processing service providers to remove barriers that prevent customers from switching to a different provider or moving data to their own systems.
- art_024.md | Article 24 | Scope of the technical obligations | This part defines which data processing services are subject to the technical obligations outlined in specific articles of the Data Act.
- art_025.md | Article 25 | Contractual terms concerning switching | This part covers the rules for written contracts between customers and data processing service providers regarding switching providers or moving to on-premises infrastructure.
- art_026.md | Article 26 | Information obligation of providers of data processing services | This section explains what information data processing service providers must share with customers about switching services, data formats, and available data exports.
- art_027.md | Article 27 | Obligation of good faith | This part explains the legal obligation for all parties to cooperate honestly and efficiently during data switching and processing.
- art_028.md | Article 28 | Contractual transparency obligations on international access and transfer | This part outlines the transparency obligations for data processing service providers regarding international access and transfer of non-personal data under the Data Act.
- art_029.md | Article 29 | Gradual withdrawal of switching charges | This part explains the rules for switching charges on data processing services, including when they must be removed and how they can be reduced before full removal.
- art_030.md | Article 30 | Technical aspects of switching | This part covers the technical requirements and obligations for providers of data processing services to facilitate customer switching between services, including functional equivalence, open interfaces, interoperability standards, and data export rules.
- art_031.md | Article 31 | Specific regime for certain data processing services | This part explains exceptions to certain data processing service obligations for custom-built or non-production services.
## CHAPTER VII — UNLAWFUL INTERNATIONAL GOVERNMENTAL ACCESS AND TRANSFER OF NON-PERSONAL DATA
- art_032.md | Article 32 | International governmental access and transfer | This part explains the rules for when and how non-personal data held in the EU can be accessed or transferred to foreign governments.
## CHAPTER VIII — INTEROPERABILITY
- art_033.md | Article 33 | Essential requirements regarding interoperability of data, of data sharing mechanisms and services, as well as of common European data spaces | This part covers the essential legal and technical requirements for ensuring interoperability in data sharing, including standards for data description, access methods, and compliance mechanisms.
- art_034.md | Article 34 | Interoperability for the purposes of in-parallel use of data processing services | This part explains additional interoperability rules for data processing services when used alongside other services, including conditions on data egress charges.
- art_035.md | Article 35 | Interoperability of data processing services | This part covers the rules and requirements for making data processing services interoperable, including standards, portability, and compliance with EU regulations.
- art_036.md | Article 36 | Essential requirements regarding smart contracts for executing data sharing agreements | This part covers the legal requirements and conformity assessment process for smart contracts used in data sharing agreements under the Data Act.
## CHAPTER IX — IMPLEMENTATION AND ENFORCEMENT
- art_037.md | Article 37 | Competent authorities and data coordinators | This part covers the roles, responsibilities, and cooperation requirements of competent authorities and data coordinators under the Data Act.
- art_038.md | Article 38 | Right to lodge a complaint | This part explains the process for filing complaints about rights violations under the Data Act, including how authorities handle and resolve them.
- art_039.md | Article 39 | Right to an effective judicial remedy | This part covers the right to challenge decisions made by authorities and the process for seeking legal remedies.
- art_040.md | Article 40 | Penalties | This part covers the penalties and enforcement measures for violations of the Data Act, including how fines are determined and imposed.
- art_041.md | Article 41 | Model contractual terms and standard contractual clauses | This part explains the Commission’s role in creating non-binding model terms and clauses to guide fair data access and cloud computing contracts.
- art_042.md | Article 42 | Role of the EDIB | This section explains the responsibilities of the European Data Innovation Board (EDIB) in supporting the enforcement and implementation of the Data Act.
## CHAPTER X — SUI GENERIS RIGHT UNDER DIRECTIVE 96/9/EC
- art_043.md | Article 43 | Databases containing certain data | This part explains when the sui generis right for databases does not apply to data from connected products or related services under the Data Act.
## CHAPTER XI — FINAL PROVISIONS
- art_044.md | Article 44 | Other Union legal acts governing rights and obligations on data access and use | This part clarifies how the Data Act interacts with other existing EU laws on data access and use.
- art_045.md | Article 45 | Exercise of the delegation | This part covers the rules and procedures for how the European Commission exercises its delegated powers under the Data Act.
- art_046.md | Article 46 | Committee procedure | This part explains how the European Commission will be supported by a specific committee when implementing the Data Act.
- art_047.md | Article 47 | Amendment to Regulation (EU) 2017/2394 | This part updates the Annex of Regulation (EU) 2017/2394 by adding a reference to the Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854).
- art_048.md | Article 48 | Amendment to Directive (EU) 2020/1828 | This part adds a reference to the Data Act regulation to an existing EU directive.
- art_049.md | Article 49 | Evaluation and review | This part covers the Commission’s evaluation and review of the Data Act, including its impact on data sharing, innovation, and enforcement, to be completed by September 2028.
- art_050.md | Article 50 | Entry into force and application | This section outlines the effective dates and application timelines for the Data Act, including when the regulation takes effect and specific rules for different types of contracts and obligations.
